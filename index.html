Our Story

Baked from the Heart

The Cakewalk With Taruna began in 2012 as a dream born in a small kitchen in Ambikapatty, Silchar — and over 13 years, that dream has grown into one of the most loved cake destinations in the region.

Taruna crafts every cake fresh to order — from elegant custom birthday creations and anniversary cakes to delicate doughnuts and cupcakes. No mass production, no shortcuts. Just honest ingredients, artistic passion, and love baked into every layer.

Eggless options always available. Home delivery across Silchar.

— Taruna
